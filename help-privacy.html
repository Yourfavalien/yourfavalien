SUPPORT PRIVACY

Your privacy when you ask for help

This page explains the information used to answer support questions, manage tickets, and operate live chat.

Information you provide

When you submit a support ticket, send an email, or start a live chat, you may provide your name, email address, page or link, support category, and a description of the problem.

How support information is used

The Support Team uses this information to answer your request, troubleshoot problems, keep a record of the conversation, prevent abuse, and improve support articles and services.

Live chat

The Support Center uses Zoho SalesIQ for live chat and the support bot. Zoho may process chat messages, contact details, device or browser information, and service data needed to operate the chat. Use the privacy choices available in the chat widget where offered.

Support tickets and email

Ticket submissions are delivered through the Support Center form service and sent to the private support inbox. Email messages are handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving them.

Cookies and local storage

Essential storage may remember chat state, security settings, or service preferences. Optional storage should follow the privacy choice selected on the site when it applies.

Sharing and retention

Support information is shared only with service providers needed to run the Support Center or when required by law. It is kept only as long as reasonably needed for support, security, recordkeeping, or legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of support information by emailing help@yourfavalien.com. Do not send passwords, login codes, payment details, or sensitive identification in a support request.

Updates

This privacy page may be updated when support tools or practices change. The latest version will remain available from the Support Center footer.
