INSPIRATION READER · PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Inspiration Reader is a proposed private, read-only integration intended to help its owner review Pinterest boards and pins for creative inspiration using ChatGPT. The integration is not yet operational. This policy describes its intended data practices; it will be updated if implementation changes.

Information accessed

With the Pinterest account owner's authorization, Inspiration Reader intends to request read-only access to boards, pins, and related board or pin metadata permitted by Pinterest. It does not request permission to publish, modify, or delete pins.

Purpose and processing

Authorized information would be used to organize and analyze creative references, including fashion, beauty, hair, and photoshoot ideas. Relevant information may be transmitted to an AI service such as OpenAI's ChatGPT to generate requested analysis, subject to that service's applicable privacy terms and settings.

Storage and retention

No Pinterest API data is currently being collected by Inspiration Reader because the integration is not yet active. Before activation, this policy will be updated to describe any server-side storage, logs, retention periods, and deletion procedures actually implemented. Information sent to third-party services may be retained under their own policies.

Sharing

The integration is intended for the account owner's personal use, not for advertising or sale of Pinterest information. Data may be processed by the service providers required to operate the integration.

Control and revocation

The account owner can revoke authorization through Pinterest's connected-app controls. Revocation stops future authorized API access; deletion of previously processed data, if any, is subject to the applicable service's deletion procedures.

Changes

This policy may change as the integration is developed. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact page on the hosting website. Inspiration Reader is a personal project separate from the YourFavAlien business.
