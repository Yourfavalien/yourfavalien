before you signal

quick fixes

a few things you can check before opening a ticket.

I subscribed but did not get an email.

Check spam, junk, and promotions folders and make sure the email address was entered correctly. If nothing arrives, open a support ticket and choose “newsletter / email.”

A page or button is not working.

Try refreshing once. If it is still broken, include the page name or URL in your ticket and describe what happened when you tapped or clicked it.

I want to change my privacy choices.

Open the site menu and choose Privacy Choices. You can also read the Privacy page for more information about how the site handles optional storage.

What should I include in a bug report?

Tell us which page you were on, what you expected to happen, and what actually happened. You do not need to include passwords or other sensitive information.
